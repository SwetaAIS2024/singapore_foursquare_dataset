Issues with the summary results generated:
1. Prompt echo 
2. Filler words like UN, POI, PAR etc
3. Under one user_id, many summaries for multiple users
4. Imaginary questions added
5. Summaries are sometimes very short as in 10 words, and sometimes too long - 70 words to 100 words
6. Names of the users are mostly Mary or John Doe
7. Frequent usage of adjectives as in Foursquare user - UserID
8. Sometimes random codes, github links and some random instructions are added

What are the possible reasons for this issue?



The issues with the results generated could stem from the following reasons:

1. **Prompt Echoing**: The model might be trained on datasets where prompts or instructions are included in the output, leading to unintended replication of the input instructions.

2. **Filler Words and Random Text**: This could result from noisy or uncleaned training data, where irrelevant or placeholder text (e.g., "UN," "POI," "PAR") was present and learned by the model.

3. **Multiple Summaries for One User**: This might occur due to improper grouping or filtering of data, where the model processes multiple unrelated entries under the same user ID.

4. **Imaginary Questions Added**: This could be due to the model being trained on datasets that include Q&A formats, causing it to generate questions even when not required.

5. **Inconsistent Summary Lengths**: The lack of clear constraints or guidelines in the training data for summary length could lead to variability in output length.

6. **Generic Names (e.g., John/Mary Doe)**: These names are often used as placeholders in training datasets, leading the model to default to them when generating user-specific content.

7. **Overuse of Adjectives**: This could result from training data that emphasizes descriptive or promotional language, leading to excessive use of adjectives.

8. **Random Code, Links, and Instructions**: This might occur if the training data included mixed content types (e.g., code snippets, documentation, or instructions) without proper filtering or separation.

### Suggestions to Address These Issues:
- **Clean Training Data**: Ensure the training data is free from irrelevant text, filler words, and placeholder content.
- **Fine-Tune the Model**: Use a curated dataset specific to the task, with clear examples of concise, structured summaries.
- **Set Output Constraints**: Define strict rules for summary length, format, and content during fine-tuning or prompt engineering.
- **Filter Input Data**: Preprocess the input data to ensure only relevant and grouped information is passed to the model.
- **Prompt Engineering**: Use precise and unambiguous prompts to guide the model toward generating the desired output.
